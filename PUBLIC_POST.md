# Proposed public post

I have built and tested the Obligation-Bound Policy Admission Lab v0.6.

It examines a narrow control problem: a policy should not acquire continuing authority merely because it was previously admitted.

Within the declared single-engine, single-writer harness:

- 59/59 attack fixtures produced the intended typed outcomes.
- Zero attack fixtures committed a governed consequence.
- The valid control committed exactly once.
- The checker-disabled control halted.

The repository binds the declared obligation, register-relative authority, derivation and approval records, external evidence contracts, checked-to-executed interpretation, current standing, activation state and final commit guard.

Claim ceiling: this does not establish safety, production readiness, global non-bypassability or legitimacy.
