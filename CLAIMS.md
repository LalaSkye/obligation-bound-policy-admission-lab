# Claim ceiling

The repository demonstrates only the behavior of its declared reference harness against its frozen fixtures and oracles.

It may claim:

1. exact proof-object and interpretation identities were bound under the configured trust inputs;
2. the evaluated predicates produced a typed `ALLOW`, `HOLD`, or `DENY`;
3. activation and commit either satisfied the declared local guards or produced a typed operational failure;
4. the 59 enumerated attacks produced their exact expected evidence in the recorded run.

It may not infer:

- source legitimacy from freshness or signature;
- substantive truth from a source contract;
- substantive derivation correctness from record integrity;
- current standing from historical admission or active state;
- safety from admission, standing, activation, enforcement, or test success;
- global non-bypassability from one enumerated harness;
- production readiness from build or release identity.

Every proof object receives only the authority of the proposition it actually establishes. No inheritance, laundering, or upgrade by proximity.
